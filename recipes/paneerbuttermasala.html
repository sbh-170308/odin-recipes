Paneer Butter Masala

Description

Paneer Butter Masala is a rich and creamy North Indian curry made with soft paneer cubes cooked in a buttery tomato-based gravy. It is mildly spiced, slightly sweet, and full of delicious flavors from butter, cream, tomatoes, and aromatic Indian spices.

This popular dish is often enjoyed with naan, roti, or jeera rice and is loved for its smooth texture and restaurant-style taste.

Ingredients

  • 200g paneer (cut into cubes)
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 cup tomato puree
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 10–12 cashew nuts
  • 1/2 cup fresh cream
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Steps

  1. Heat butter and oil in a pan.
  2. Add cumin seeds and chopped onion.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.
  4. Add tomato puree and cook until the oil separates.
  5. Add paneer cubes and cook for 2-3 minutes.
  6. Stir in fresh cream and garam masala.
  7. Garnish with kasuri methi and fresh coriander leaves.
