Smooth Sweet Tea

Description

Tea is a refreshing and popular beverage enjoyed by people all over the world.

It is easy to prepare and perfect for relaxing mornings or evenings.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon tea powder
  • 2 teaspoons sugar

Steps

  1. Boil water in a pan.
  2. Add tea powder and sugar.
  3. Pour milk into the pan.
  4. Boil for 2 to 3 minutes.
  5. Strain the tea into a cup and serve hot.
